How the baseline_section_sha256 digests in evals/cases.jsonl case dz-07 were produced.

Subject: skills/designing-interfaces/evals/fixtures/ui-003-approved.md, the file the
runner copies to .devforgeai/ui-specs/UI-003.md before the case runs.

Recipe, implemented once as _section_digest in evals/graders.py and applied to the
sections of that fixture to produce the values below:

  1. Drop the YAML frontmatter: the body is everything below the closing '---'.
  2. Split the body on lines starting with '## '. A section's text is the lines
     below its heading line and above the next '## ' heading.
  3. Strip trailing whitespace from every line.
  4. Drop leading and trailing blank lines.
  5. Join the remaining lines with a single '\n'. No trailing newline.
  6. Encode UTF-8 and take the SHA-256 hex digest, lowercase.

The grader recomputes the digest of each section of the file the run left behind and
compares it to the recorded value, so the recipe and the recorded values come from one
function and cannot drift apart.
